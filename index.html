Guided by Faith • United in Purpose • Built on Values

Strive To Make Tima A Model Village

YCA Inc. is committed to working together and impoving lives through Education, Business Development, Health, Cultural Events Celebrations, Church Partnership, and Sustainable Projects that support social, spiritual, and economic well-being.

Our Motto

As for Yesup Tribe, we will serve the Lord (Joshua 24:15).

Our Vision

To build a united, empowered, peaceful, and progressive community that upholds equality, sustainable development, and conflict resolution through dialogue and mutual understanding to make Tima a Model Village.

Our Mission

To serve and empower community through living Godly principles and prioritizing education, business, and work garden, focusing on inclusive programs, capacity-building, sustainable and innovative development initiatives, agriculture, and community engagement.

As part of its commitment to self-advancement and community development, Yesup Community Association (YCA) Inc. has initiated a range of impactful programs aimed at improving the social, environmental, and educational well-being of the community. These include uprooting marijuana to combat illegal drug activity, conducting road clean-ups to maintain safe and hygienic public spaces, implementing community decoration projects to enhance shared areas, organizing tree planting initiatives to promote environmental conservation, establishing Early Childhood Education centers to provide quality education for young children, and constructing public toilets to improve sanitation and hygiene. Through these efforts, YCA Inc. empowers community members to actively participate in building a safer, healthier, and more sustainable environment.
