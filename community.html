Community Engagement/Service

The Yesup Community Association Inc. implements a series of strategic community services that align with its development vision and the National Goals and Directive Principles of Papua New Guinea. These projects aim to promote social progress, economic empowerment, environmental sustainability, and community well-being. Each project reflects the Association’s commitment to inclusive development through participatory planning and community ownership.

The Association approach integrates education, health, environment, faith, and business initiatives to ensure holistic and sustainable outcomes. Through these programs, the Association works in partnership with local institutions, schools, churches, and development organizations to improve the quality of life for all members of the community. The focus is on empowering individuals and families to take active roles in development while fostering accountability, self-reliance, and respect for cultural and spiritual values.
