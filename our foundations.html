Our Foundations

The foundation of the Yesup Community Association Inc. stands firmly on faith, unity, and service. Guided by powerful words of wisdom and the principles of compassion and integrity, YCA’s values define who we are as a community. These slogans and core values serve as a moral compass that inspires every action we take — to empower lives, strengthen families, and build a Model Village grounded in peace, love, and respect.

Our Core Values

Unity

Promoting peace, inclusiveness, and cooperation among all members of the community to thrive together.

Respect

Upholding cultural values, honoring elders, and treating everyone with dignity and understanding.

Integrity

Encouraging honesty, accountability, and ethical conduct in leadership and daily life.

Self-Reliance

Encouraging initiative, hard work, and contribution to the village’s development and sustainability.

Sustainability

Protecting natural resources through responsible environmental practices for future generations.

Equality

Ensuring all voices are heard — women, youth, children, people with disabilities, and minority groups.
