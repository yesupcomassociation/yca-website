Our Community Events

Annual Christmas Crusade

A yearly spiritual gathering held during December to strengthen faith, foster unity, and reflect on community values through worship and fellowship.

Independence Day Celebrations

A yearly celebration of the PNG Independence Day on the 16th of September to honor the great patriarchs and heros behind PNG's freedom.

Sports

Organized tournaments and sports days that encourage youth participation, teamwork, discipline, and healthy living across all age groups.

Cultural Celebrations

These events showcase the diverse traditions, dances, and heritage of our communities, promoting pride and respect for our cultural identity.

World Health Day

Observed each year to raise awareness on important health issues, encourage healthy lifestyles, and support public health campaigns in our communities.

World Environment Day

Celebrated through clean-up programs, tree planting, and awareness campaigns to encourage environmental stewardship and sustainability.
