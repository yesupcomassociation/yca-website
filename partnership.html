Our Partners & Collaborations

The Yesup Community Association Inc. values collaboration and partnership with government agencies, NGOs, churches, and development organizations to achieve sustainable community growth and empowerment.

Pomberal-Topua Junior High School

Supporting secondary education and student development through community-based sponsorship and mentoring programs.

New Pomberal Association Inc.

Building strong networks and cooperation among local associations to enhance development impact.

Pomberal Primary School

Collaborating to improve educational outcomes and create opportunities for young learners in the community.

PNG University of Technology (UNITECH)

Collaborating through the Appropriate Technology and Community Development Institute (ATCDI) for our community innovation projects.

Catholic & Other Churches

Working together in faith-based programs, social transformation, and moral development of young people.

Pomberal Health Centre

Promoting awareness on nutrition, hygiene, maternal and child health, and disease prevention in local communities.
